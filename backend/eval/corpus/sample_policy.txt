Company Refund Policy

1. Refund Eligibility
Customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase.
Refunds are available for products in original condition.
Digital products are non-refundable after download.

2. Refund Process
Contact support@company.com with your order number.
Allow 5-7 business days for processing.
Refunds will be issued to the original payment method.

3. Exceptions
Sale items are final sale and cannot be refunded.
Shipping costs are non-refundable.
Custom orders cannot be refunded.

4. Warranty
All products come with a 1-year warranty.
Warranty covers manufacturing defects only.
Physical damage is not covered.
