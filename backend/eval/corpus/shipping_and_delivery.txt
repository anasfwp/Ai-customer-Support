Shipping and Delivery Guide

Shipping Methods
We offer three shipping methods for domestic orders. Standard Ground arrives in 3-5 business days and costs $6.95. Expedited arrives in 2 business days and costs $14.95. Overnight Priority arrives the next business day when the order is placed before 1 PM EST and costs $29.95.

Free Shipping
Orders over $75 qualify for free Standard Ground shipping within the continental United States. Free shipping does not apply to Alaska, Hawaii, or US territories, and it cannot be combined with Expedited or Overnight Priority.

International Shipping
We ship to over 50 countries. International orders take 7-14 business days. Customers are responsible for all import duties, customs fees, and local taxes charged by the destination country. Orders shipped to the European Union are sent Delivered Duty Paid (DDP), so EU customers pay no additional fees on delivery.

Order Processing
Orders placed before 1 PM EST on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed after the cutoff, on weekends, or on public holidays ship the next business day. During the November holiday peak, processing can take up to 2 extra business days.

Tracking Your Order
A tracking number is emailed as soon as your package leaves our warehouse in Columbus, Ohio. Tracking information can take up to 24 hours to appear on the carrier website. You can also find tracking under Account > Orders.

Lost or Damaged Packages
If tracking shows a package as delivered but you cannot find it, wait 48 hours and check with neighbors before contacting us. Damaged packages must be reported within 7 days of delivery with photos of the box and the item. We will send a free replacement or issue a full refund for packages lost or damaged in transit.

Changing an Address
You can change the shipping address only before the order ships. Once a package is in transit, address changes must be requested directly with the carrier. We cannot ship to PO boxes using Overnight Priority.

Shipping Restrictions
Lithium battery packs over 100Wh cannot be shipped internationally or by air. Orders containing these items will automatically ship by Standard Ground only.
